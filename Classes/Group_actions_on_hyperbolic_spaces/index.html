Learning outcomes

Upon successful completion of the course, students will be able to describe examples of hyperbolic spaces and their isometry groups; explain Gromov hyperbolicity and verify it in standard examples; analyze group actions using orbits and stabilizers; classify isometries; apply the Milnor–Švarc lemma; use boundaries of hyperbolic spaces; and compare hyperbolic, relatively hyperbolic, and acylindrically hyperbolic group actions.

Course content

This course provides an introduction to group actions on hyperbolic spaces, with a focus on geometric group theory. We begin with rank-one symmetric spaces, particularly real hyperbolic space, and discuss their isometry groups and basic geometric features. We then examine Gromov hyperbolic spaces and hyperbolic groups, including quasi-isometries, the Milnor–Švarc lemma, the stability of quasigeodesics, boundaries, and examples. The second part of the course introduces relatively hyperbolic groups and their actions on cusped and coned-off spaces. Finally, we discuss acylindrically hyperbolic groups, loxodromic elements, and examples arising from mapping class groups, outer automorphism groups of free groups, and groups acting on CAT(0) cube complexes.
